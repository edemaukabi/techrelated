Admissions

We welcome students from all backgrounds. Our straightforward admissions process is designed to help you take the first step toward an outstanding education.

How to Apply

01

Submit Online Application

Complete our simple online form with your personal details and academic history.

02

Upload Documents

Provide transcripts, recommendation letters, and a personal statement.

03

Entrance Assessment

Attend our placement test and informal interview with department heads.

04

Receive Decision

Get your admission decision within 10 business days and enroll online.

Key Dates — 2026/27

  • Applications Open Jan 15, 2026
  • Early Decision Deadline Mar 1, 2026
  • Regular Deadline May 15, 2026
  • Decisions Released Jun 1, 2026
  • Term Begins Sep 3, 2026
Apply Now

Requirements

  • Completed secondary education (WAEC/NECO or equivalent)
  • Minimum of 5 credits including English and Mathematics
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Personal statement (500–800 words)
